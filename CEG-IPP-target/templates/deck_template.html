Strategic Recommendations

13

Leverage Strengths

  • Maximize nuclear uptime — the 45U PTC makes every nuclear MWh worth $15/MWh more; prioritize license extensions and uprates
  • Pursue 24/7 CFE contracts — nuclear baseload + geographic diversity enables premium hourly-matching PPAs
  • Expand clean energy portfolio — pair existing nuclear with solar/storage to offer full-spectrum clean energy

Mitigate Risks

  • Accelerate coal exit — remaining coal exposure creates regulatory and financial risk; prioritize orderly retirement
  • Hedge gas peaker exposure — transition peakers to battery storage or dual-fuel configurations
  • Monitor capacity market evolution — PJM capacity revenues are a significant revenue stream; track reform proposals
Bottom line: Constellation is the best-positioned US IPP for the energy transition, with the largest zero-carbon fleet, geographic reach, and regulatory tailwinds. The primary risk is complacency — competitors are investing aggressively in renewables and storage.

Appendix: Methodology

14

Data Sources

  • Fleet data: EIA-860 (capacity), EIA-923 (generation), eGRID 2022 (emissions)
  • Scenario modeling: Hourly CFE Optimizer — SMARTargets IPP sweep module
  • Cost assumptions: NREL ATB 2024, Lazard LCOE v17, pipeline_config.py

Spider Map Scoring

  • Nuclear Advantage: Nuclear capacity / total capacity × 200 (capped at 100)
  • Geographic Diversity: Number of ISOs × 25 (capped at 100)
  • Coal Exit Progress: 100 − (coal capacity / total capacity × 200)
  • Gas Flexibility: CCGT / (CCGT + Peaker) capacity × 100
  • Clean Pipeline: (Solar + Wind + Battery) / total capacity × 300 (capped at 100)

Scenario Parameters

  • Demand growth: Low / Medium / High
  • Price sensitivity: Multiple wholesale price scenarios
  • PPA levels: Low / Medium / High contract coverage
  • Gas transition friction: 0.3 / 0.7 / 1.0 (probability of gas asset stranding)
Full methodology: Hourly CFE Optimizer documentation · github.com/jessicacohen554-cyber/hourly-cfe-optimizer
