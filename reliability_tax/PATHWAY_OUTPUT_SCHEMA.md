## Keys removed from v1

- **`terminal_new_gas_fleet`** — byte-identical to `tables.new_gas_fleet` in every v1 payload. Single source of truth is `tables.new_gas_fleet`.
- **`tables.stranding_ledger`** — encoded the Card K comparative-vs-Pathway-3 overbuild delta. Card K' supersedes with absolute (per-pathway) stranding in `stranding_metadata.total_stranded_capex_usd`.
- **`config.q45`** — 45Q tax credit toggle. v2 assumes it's always on; no on/off modeling.
- **`feasibility.economic`** — Card J $/CFE% economic-floor diagnostic. v2 reports true ratepayer cost; doesn't opine on whether that cost is "economic."
- **`tables.new_gas_fleet[0].annual_cf`** — duplicate of per-year `tables.annual_cost[y].gas_fleet_cf`.
- **`tables.new_gas_fleet[0].recovered_revenue_per_kw`** — Card F' revenue accumulator; no longer drives stranding under Card K' peak-year-snapshot rule.
