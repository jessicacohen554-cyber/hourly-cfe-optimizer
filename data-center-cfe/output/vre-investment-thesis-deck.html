Investment Thesis Summary

Demand is structural, not cyclical. AI inference scales linearly with adoption. The 231 GW US DC pipeline is committed capital with 3–5 year build horizons.
The green premium is real and liquid. Hyperscalers are executing multi-GW deals at $35–50/MWh premiums above market. This isn’t ESG posturing — it’s a financial hedge.
Storage is the unlock. Pairing 4–8hr BESS with VRE transforms the revenue stack (+169%), captures capacity value, and meets the 24/7 CFE product that buyers demand.
The window is finite. VRE+Storage is the only technology that can deploy at scale in the 2025–2032 bridge period. First movers capture interconnection scarcity and long-term offtake.

Procurement Accounting: What It Means for Where to Buy

9

Three competing procurement strategies are fragmenting the market — but the VRE+Storage investment thesis holds under all of them. Strategy choice shapes where to weight the portfolio, not whether to invest.

Three Strategies, One Thesis

① Consequential (Cross-Regional)
Buy cheapest $/tCO₂ clean energy anywhere in the US. No hourly constraint. Buyer picks lowest-cost ISO. Drives new-build in MISO/SPP/ERCOT where wind is cheapest.
Buyers: Meta, Amazon (large MISO/SPP unbundled REC purchases). Incentive: Cheapest path to high CFE % — VRE pipeline in wind-rich ISOs is the product.
② Hourly Matching (Same-ISO)
Match consumption with clean generation every hour, same ISO. Forces storage + firm pairing. Requires 1.7× over-procurement at 95% CFE to cover nights and low-wind.
Buyers: Google (24/7 CFE), Microsoft (nuclear PPAs). Incentive: VRE+Storage is the only product that satisfies hourly matching — mandatory pairing = higher $/MWh but guaranteed offtake.
③ Annual Volumetric (Status Quo)
Match total MWh over the year. Cheapest unbundled RECs satisfy targets — high paper CFE %, low emissions impact. Being phased out by leading buyers.
Incentive: Minimal VRE pipeline value — existing RECs suffice. Sunset strategy as SBTi/SEC tighten standards.

The MISO Question: How Much Committed Load Makes It Work?

Meta + Amazon = the demand signal. Both companies use consequential accounting. MISO VRE pipeline value depends on whether their committed DC load actually materializes and drives procurement:
  • 5% C&I participation (hyperscaler-only): 17 TWh clean demand → ~7 GW new VRE at $49/MWh — still buyable from existing pool
  • 10% participation (+ corporate followers): 34 TWh → ~13 GW VRE. Still $49/MWh. Pipeline value emerging but not dominant.
  • 20–30% (broad adoption): 69–103 TWh → existing pool exhausts. New-build wind + solar required. This is the inflection point where MISO VRE pipeline becomes a must-own.
Strategy-proof portfolio (PJM, ERCOT, NYISO — 80% weight): VRE+Storage holds value regardless of which accounting method dominates.
Strategy-leveraged hedge (MISO, SPP — 15–20%): Upside if consequential persists + Meta/Amazon load materializes. Downside protected by cheap basis (pivot to storage-paired if hourly wins).
Bottom line: The market will adopt a hybrid of strategies — Google hourly, Meta consequential, most corporates annual. Fragmentation increases total VRE demand. Storage pairing is essential under both strategies that matter (hourly + consequential at scale). Different companies, different methods, same conclusion: build VRE+Storage.
