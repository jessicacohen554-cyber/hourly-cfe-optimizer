Where Does Each Scenario Lead?

Eight scenarios model different futures for the US power sector. Reference scenarios (R1/R2) show market-only outcomes. Ambitious Transition scenarios (AT1–4) enforce emission caps toward net-zero. Qualified Targets (QT1–4) sweep 19 reduction levels (5–95%) to find the cost-effective frontier, creating probability fans that reveal the range of possible outcomes.
