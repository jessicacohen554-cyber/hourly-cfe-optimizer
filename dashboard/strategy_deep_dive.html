Key Takeaways

Strategy choice matters enormously. The difference between the most and least effective procurement strategies spans hundreds of GW of avoided gas capacity, tens of Mt of CO₂, and tens of billions of dollars in learning-curve acceleration.

Hourly matching drives the deepest decarbonization. Track 2 strategies (especially 2C with SSS allocation) consistently achieve the highest grid clean percentages and gas displacement at moderate participation levels.

Annual matching is cheapest but least impactful. Track 3 strategies offer the lowest buyer cost but rely heavily on existing credits, producing minimal new-build additionality and limited grid transformation.

Scale unlocks learning curves. At sufficient participation (25–40%), Wright’s Law effects drive clean firm technology costs below fossil alternatives, creating a self-reinforcing deployment cycle. The question is which strategy gets there fastest.
