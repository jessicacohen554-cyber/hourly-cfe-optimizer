The Reliability Tax

What you pay when reliability is treated as a residual, not a design objective.

Grids can be clean, affordable, and reliable — but only if all three are designed for. When planning optimizes for clean and affordable and assumes reliability will just happen, reliability is paid for implicitly through a redundant fossil fleet that has to stay online to cover the hours solar, wind, and storage can’t economically serve. This analysis puts a number on that hidden cost across seven US ISOs and five 2050 CFE endpoints.

The reliability tax = the fixed costs of the gas fleet that must remain online to fill hours VRE and storage cannot economically serve, net of capacity market revenues, expressed as $/MWh of total consumption.
