Storage Analysis

How battery and long-duration storage reshape the decarbonization path under demand growth

Storage plays two fundamentally different roles depending on the grid's starting point. In solar-heavy ISOs like CAISO and NYISO, 4-hour batteries appear early — daily cycling to shift midday surplus into evening peaks. In wind-heavy ISOs (ERCOT, MISO, SPP), storage barely registers until ≥95% because wind overbuild is cheaper than storing energy. Long-duration storage (100hr iron-air) only enters in the last mile (≥99%) for multi-day weather gaps. This page compares Track 1 baseline (existing grid + procurement) and Track 2 demand growth scenarios.
