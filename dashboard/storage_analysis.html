Storage Analysis

Battery and long-duration storage in the optimal mix across 7 ISOs

4-hour Li-ion is the default storage workhorse, appearing from moderate thresholds onward in solar-heavy ISOs. LDES (100hr iron-air) enters only at ≥99% in wind-heavy regions. 8-hour batteries play a marginal niche role. Green hydrogen is never cost-optimal.
