The 8,760 Problem

Optimal clean energy targets and no-regrets investments across US power markets

Every region has an optimal clean energy target

Grid decarbonization is cheaper than Direct Air Capture — up to a point. That point varies by region and depends on technology costs. The crossover range below shows where each region's marginal abatement cost exceeds projected DAC costs across 5,832 cost scenarios. Within that range, certain resource investments pay off under any plausible cost future.

Key findings across regions

Grid decarbonization beats DAC to at least 70–97.5% in every region under medium cost assumptions. The spread is driven by two factors: renewable resource quality (solar/wind capacity factors) and firm generation costs (nuclear, geothermal, CCS).

Firm generation investment is the single largest lever. Cheap nuclear or geothermal shifts the crossover 10–15 percentage points higher in constrained regions (NEISO, NYISO). Renewable tax credits add 2–5 points. Storage costs contribute less than 1% of variance everywhere.

The no-regrets investments identified below represent resources that appear in the optimal portfolio across the entire crossover range and under all 5,832 cost sensitivity combinations — they should be built regardless of how cost uncertainty resolves.

Methodology

Crossover Analysis

The optimal CFE target for each region is determined by the crossover point where the marginal cost of grid decarbonization exceeds projected Direct Air Capture (DAC) costs. We compute this using stepwise marginal abatement costs (MAC) from the Step 3 cost optimization — the change in clean procurement cost divided by the change in CO₂ abated between adjacent thresholds.

The crossover range spans 9 combinations: 3 grid cost tiers (P10/P50/P90 from 5,832 sensitivity scenarios) crossed with 3 DAC trajectories (optimistic, central, conservative). This captures uncertainty in both clean energy costs and DAC cost learning curves.

No-Regrets Resource Identification

For each threshold within the crossover range, the optimizer evaluates all 5,832 cost sensitivity scenarios (3 renewable gen × 3 firm gen × 3 battery × 3 LDES × 3 fossil fuel × 4 transmission × 3 CCS × 2 45Q settings) and identifies the cheapest feasible resource portfolio. The demand growth dimension adds 3 growth levels (Low/Medium/High) per scenario.

A resource is classified as:

  • No-regrets (≥80% presence): appears in the optimal portfolio across 80%+ of all scenario-threshold-growth combinations
  • Likely (50–80% presence): appears in a majority but not all cost scenarios
  • Scenario-dependent (<50% presence): only optimal under specific cost assumptions

The floor (minimum allocation across all scenarios) represents the absolute minimum investment needed regardless of how costs resolve. The average represents the expected investment level.
