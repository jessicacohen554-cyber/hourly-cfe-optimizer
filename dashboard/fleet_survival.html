Key finding: Under the SBTi trajectory, coal fleet capacity across all tracked companies reaches zero by ~2033 (60% clean threshold). Gas peakers follow next, with most curtailed by ~2040. Efficient gas CCGTs are the last fossil assets standing, declining gradually through 2050. Companies with heavy coal exposure (Vistra, NRG) face the steepest near-term stranding risk.

Methodology: Survival rates are computed from the Track 1 (Existing Clean Floor) baseline pipeline's merit-order fossil retirement model. Coal is displaced first, then oil, then gas. Gas CCGTs dispatch before peakers (lower heat rate / marginal cost). P10/P90 bounds reflect demand growth uncertainty (0.5%/yr low, 1.0%/yr medium, 2.0%/yr high). Company fleet data sourced from EIA Form 860/923, eGRID, company sustainability/investor reports, and MJ Bradley/ERM Benchmarking Air Emissions (21st ed., 2025). CO2 estimates use eGRID regional emission rates by fuel type.
