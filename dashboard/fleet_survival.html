Methodology

Company fleet data from EIA Form 860/923, eGRID, company sustainability reports, and MJ Bradley/ERM Benchmarking Air Emissions (21st ed., 2025). Constellation includes Calpine fleet post-acquisition. Dispatch from Track 1 baseline merit-order retirement model (Step 4/PP4). Gas dispatch split CCGT vs peaker via merit-order priority: CCGTs dispatch first (7.0 MMBtu/MWh heat rate vs CT 10.5). Coal nameplate removed when regional coal dispatch reaches zero (physical retirement). Gas nameplate held constant — economic retirement modeled at ~15% CF threshold. SBTi timeline: 50% clean = 2030 through 100% = 2050.
