05

Nuclear Preservation Test

Existing nuclear provides ~50% of US clean electricity. Losing it would be catastrophic for grid decarbonization. Strategy 2C creates EAC revenue that keeps nuclear plants economically viable. Strategy 1B provides zero nuclear support — under 1B, nuclear faces the same merchant revenue erosion with no offsetting demand. This test quantifies what happens to the grid if nuclear retires, and underscores why 2C's nuclear support is a feature, not a fragility.
