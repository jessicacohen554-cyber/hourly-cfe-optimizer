Three independent modeling approaches — parametric market sweeps, structured net-zero pathways, and cost-optimized baselines — stress-test the IPP nuclear fleet under hundreds of scenarios to identify which risks are real, which are model artifacts, and which findings hold universally.

Model B: Net-Zero Transition Pathways (10 Scenarios) Power Sector & Economy-Wide NZ

Structured transition scenarios from Step 10 model four net-zero trajectories: Power Sector NZ (AT1/AT2), Economy-Wide NZ (AT3/AT4), plus Queue-Driven (QT1-4) and Reference (R1/R2) pathways. Each defines an ISO-level LMP and clean penetration trajectory through 2050.

Model C: Cost-Optimized Baseline + Capacity Adders (90 Scenarios) Step 3A Track 1

Uses Step 3A's 5,832-scenario cost optimization to derive wholesale electricity prices and capacity market signals at various clean penetration thresholds (50–99%). This captures the "price cannibalization" effect: as clean energy grows, wholesale prices fall, eroding nuclear merchant revenue.
