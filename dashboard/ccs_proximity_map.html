Spatial analysis of 39 natural gas combined cycle plants scored for CCUS retrofit potential based on CO₂ emissions, proximity to pipeline infrastructure, Class VI injection wells, and geologic storage formations.

How We Score CCS Viability

Each plant receives a composite viability score (0–100) based on four spatial and emissions factors. Higher scores indicate better candidates for CCUS retrofitting — plants that emit more CO₂, sit closer to existing transport infrastructure, and overlie proven storage geology.
