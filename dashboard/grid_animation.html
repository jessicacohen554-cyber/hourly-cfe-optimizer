Winter Storm Uri

The worst grid crisis in ERCOT history. Over 30 GW of generation went offline as temperatures plunged below 0°F. Rolling blackouts affected 4.5 million customers. Watch the cascade unfold hour by hour.

Extreme heat drove demand near 85 GW. Low wind during peak afternoon hours stressed reserves. See how solar and gas compensate when wind dies down.

The grid operating as designed. Strong wind and solar, moderate demand, comfortable reserves. The baseline for what "normal" looks like.
