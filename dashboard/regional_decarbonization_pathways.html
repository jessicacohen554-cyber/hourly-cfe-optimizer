Regional Decarbonization Pathways

Where the market takes each grid, what the gap to net-zero looks like, and what it takes to close it

The market will not deliver net-zero on its own. Clean energy deployment is profitable up to a point — then it stalls. The gap between where market forces take each region and where it needs to be for net-zero defines the scale and cost of policy intervention required. Each region starts from a different grid, faces different constraints, and needs a different portfolio to close the gap. Select a region to explore its pathway.
