Act 1

The Grid is Not a Bank

The grid is a live, interconnected machine where carbon intensity varies wildly by the hour and location. Most clean energy policies ignore this physics, treating all megawatt-hours as equal.

A solar MWh generated at noon in California displaces almost no fossil fuel—the grid is already clean. That same MWh at 8 PM in PJM displaces coal. Annual accounting treats them as identical. Hourly data reveals the truth.

Act 2

The 100% Clean Illusion

Annual accounting matches abundant daytime solar with nighttime demand. The math works on paper, but physically, the company relies entirely on the dirty grid after dark.

Consider a CAISO buyer who procures enough wind and solar to cover 100% of annual load. Spreadsheet says: mission accomplished. But look at any single hour—solar generates nothing at midnight, and wind can’t fill the gap alone.

The chart shows a real 24-hour dispatch profile from our optimizer. Watch the “balanced” average dissolve into the actual hourly shape—daytime surplus spills onto the grid while nighttime demand pulls from fossil plants.

The annual ledger balances. The atmosphere does not.
