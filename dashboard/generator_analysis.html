Why Generator Analysis Matters

Corporate clean energy procurement doesn't happen in a vacuum. Every MWh of clean energy that displaces fossil generation changes the economics of the entire fleet. Coal plants retire first — they're the highest-cost, highest-emission generators. Gas CCGTs follow as their capacity factors erode below economic viability (~15% CF threshold). The sequence and timing of these transitions vary dramatically by region.

ERCOT's wind-rich grid displaces gas faster than PJM's nuclear-heavy grid. CAISO's solar dominance creates unique storage requirements. NEISO's winter gas dependence adds pipeline constraints that affect CCS economics. Understanding these regional dynamics is essential for corporate buyers evaluating where and how to procure clean energy.
