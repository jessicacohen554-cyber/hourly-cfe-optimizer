Regional Decarbonization Pathways

Where the market takes each grid, what the gap to net-zero looks like, and what it takes to close it

The market will not deliver net-zero on its own. Clean energy deployment is profitable up to a point — then it stalls. The gap between where market forces take each region and where it needs to be for net-zero defines the scale and cost of policy intervention required. This analysis now includes 540 market scenarios per sweep type (adding an IRA policy toggle), announced coal retirements through 2035, transmission constraint modeling, RGGI carbon pricing, state RPS deployment floors, and a regulatory feedback mechanism that dampens unrealistic sustained high LMP. Each region starts from a different grid, faces different constraints, and needs a different portfolio to close the gap. Select a region to explore its pathway.
