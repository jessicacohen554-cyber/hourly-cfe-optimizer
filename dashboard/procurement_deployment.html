How to read this page

This is a cost optimization model for corporate voluntary procurement, not a market simulation. It shows what happens in a vacuum when different procurement strategies are applied at varying participation levels, based purely on LCOE and capital costs.

In reality, market dynamics drive a broader mix of new generation - we explore those trajectories in the supply-side regional climate transition analysis. Here, the goal is to isolate the demand-pull signal: which resources do these strategies incentivize, where, and at what cost?

Demand grows with ambition. Each CFE target maps to an SBTi-aligned deployment year (50% → 2030, 90% → 2040, ≥99.99% → 2050). Demand scales at ISO-specific growth rates from a 2025 baseline. Higher targets = later years = more demand to serve.

Data Sources & Methodology

Demand - 2025 baseline demand (TWh) per ISO, grown at region-specific annual rates indexed to SBTi target years. Medium growth rates: CAISO 1.9%, ERCOT 3.5%, PJM 2.4%, NYISO 2.0%, NEISO 1.8%, MISO 2.2%, SPP 1.8%.

Hourly profiles - Generation and demand shapes from 2021–2025 weather-year average (EIA Form 930). Profile shapes held constant across target years; total TWh scales with demand growth.

Offshore wind - NREL National Offshore Wind dataset (NOW-23), 2 km resolution, 2000–2020 historical record. Applied to CAISO, PJM, NYISO, and NEISO. Not modeled for ERCOT, MISO, or SPP.

Other sources - Solar: NREL TMY3. Onshore wind: EIA/NREL. Hydro: historical inflow-weighted. Emission factors: EPA eGRID. Cost assumptions: NREL ATB 2024, Lazard LCOE+ v17.
