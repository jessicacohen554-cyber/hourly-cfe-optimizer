2050 Fleet Outcome: Retrofit vs. DAC vs. Stranded

NEISO: No geologic storage. Constellation's ~2 GW NEISO gas fleet (Fore River, Granite Ridge, Westbrook) cannot retrofit with CCS due to crystalline bedrock geology. These assets face stranding or DAC offset as the only decarbonization paths.

CAISO CCS cap binding. California's 25 TWh geologic storage cap limits CCS retrofits to ~3.4 GW at 85% CF. With 4.6 GW of eligible CAISO gas, ~1.2 GW must find alternatives (retirement or DAC).
