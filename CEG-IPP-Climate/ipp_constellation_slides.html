Key Takeaways

  • Nuclear-anchored clean fleet — Constellation's nuclear base provides a strong low-carbon foundation, making their 100% carbon-free by 2040 target credible.
  • Gas lock-in is the critical decision point — New gas development would lock in emissions for decades. Corporate PPA signals can tip the balance toward clean alternatives.
  • Scenario convergence by 2035 — Most scenarios converge on significant emission reductions by mid-decade, suggesting near-term milestones are achievable.
  • Enabling conditions matter — Gaps to SMARTargets AT and SBTi v2 pathways narrow substantially with policy support for clean firm generation and hourly matching.
  • Clean investment economics are favorable — LCOE trends support accelerated renewable + storage deployment across Constellation's operating ISOs.
