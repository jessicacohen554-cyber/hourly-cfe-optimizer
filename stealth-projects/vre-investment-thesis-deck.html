LMP Cannibalization: The Standalone VRE Trap

5

The Three-Headed Challenge

Price Cannibalization

Zero-marginal-cost generation drives wholesale prices toward $0 during peak production. CAISO solar captures <30% of average LMP; ERCOT wind at 54%.

Basis Risk

Nodal price divergence from congestion means power generated in cheap zones can’t reach load. Hyperscalers now discount PPAs for basis exposure.

ELCC Collapse

PJM assigns solar ELCC of ~8%. A 100 MW solar farm gets paid for 8 MW of capacity. Standalone VRE capacity value is effectively zero.

Bottom line: A merchant standalone solar asset modeling standard forward curves without adjusting for capture-rate degradation will fail its DSCR and equity IRR hurdles. The standalone VRE investment thesis is structurally broken.

The 24/7 CFE Premium: Why Hyperscalers Will Pay More

6

From Annual Matching to Hourly CFE

The shift from annual volumetric matching to 24/7 hourly CFE is creating a structural pricing premium that fundamentally changes VRE economics.

Annual matching: Buy RECs to offset total consumption. Allows fossil reliance at night. Low cost, low impact.
24/7 hourly CFE: Match consumption with clean generation every hour. Requires storage to cover night/low-wind. Higher cost, real emissions reduction.

Validated by Landmark Deals

  • Microsoft + Constellation: 835 MW Three Mile Island nuclear restart — 20-year firm PPA at premium pricing
  • Google + Broadwing: 400 MW gas + CCS — paying for both power AND carbon capture
  • Google + AES: 24/7 CFE portfolios using solar + wind + storage arrays
  • Meta + Constellation: 20-year Clinton nuclear PPA for firm, 24/7 clean baseload
  • Amazon + Talen: 1.92 GW Susquehanna nuclear — largest single-asset nuclear PPA
Product Availability Paradox (Eurelectric/EY): Extreme complexity of structuring 24/7 CFE products creates supply scarcity → higher margins for developers who can deliver.
