Step 0 — Data Ingestion & ConfigurationPre-pipeline
0.1 — EIA-930 Hourly Data
eia_data_io.py
Ingests EIA Hourly Electric Grid Monitor data (2021–2025) for 7 ISOs. 8,760-hour normalized demand and generation profiles by fuel type. Multi-year averaging smooths weather anomalies. Nuclear monthly CF derate from NRC. Solar DST-aware nighttime zeroing.
Out: eia_generation_profiles.parquet, eia_demand_profiles.parquet
0.2 — Pipeline Configuration
pipeline_config.py
Single source of truth: 7 ISOs, 21 thresholds, LCOE tables (L/M/H × 7 ISOs × 10 resources), transmission adders, FOAK/NOAK costs, Wright’s Law parameters, demand growth rates, storage parameters, grid mix shares, capacity caps. All scripts import from here.
Sources: NREL ATB 2024, Lazard v17-18, EIA AEO 2025, EPA eGRID, LBNL

Step 1 — Physics Feasible Space Generation~3–8 hours (compute-heavy)
1.1a — Generate Mixes
step1_1a_generate_mixes.py
Cartesian product at 5% step. 4D ISOs: ~12K combos. 6D CAISO: ~1.6M. Prior-window narrowing saves ~30%. Adds seed combos from prior EF research.
1.1b — Score Mixes
step1_1b_score_mixes.py
Vectorized 8,760-hour matching in 20K-mix chunks (peak ~1.4 GiB). Score = ∑ min(1, clean_supply[h] / demand[h]) / 8760.
1.2 — Zone Search
step1_2_zone_search.py
Three score-band zones (A: 50–70%, B: 70–90%, C: 90–100%). 1% fine grid. Collision-free int64 hash dedup. Dominance filter per threshold.
1.3 — Floor-Aware PFS
step1_3_floor_aware_pfs.py
Incremental additions above existing grid floor. Solar 0–80% (2% step), wind 0–80%, clean firm 0–40%. Hydro fixed. Targets 50–70%.
1.4 — Fine Grid PFS
step1_4_fine_grid_pfs.py
1% grid for 40–70% thresholds. Solar 0–60%, wind 0–60%, CF 0–30%. Offshore 0–20% (2%), geo 0–15% (3%, CAISO).
1.5 — Storage Refinement ★
step1_5_storage_refinement.py
Pass 0: Ceiling storage (bat4=0.10%, bat8=0.15%, LDES=1.0%, H&sub2;=1.0% of demand). Eliminates mixes that can’t reach any threshold. Pass 1: Group by gap buckets (0–5pp, 5–10pp, 10–25pp, 25–50pp). Right-sized storage grid per bucket. Pass 2: 0.05% resolution near boundaries. Dominance filter after each pass.

Step 2 — Efficient Frontier & Cost Optimization~5–15 min per ISO
2.1 — Efficient Frontier
step2_1_efficient_frontier.py
Phase 1: Threshold gate — keep rows with scores in target ranges.
Phase 2: Resource cap filter — solar cap (100%), total procurement (350%), hydro (existing).
Phase 3: Global dedup — for each unique allocation, keep highest score. Each physical configuration stored once. No dominance removal across mixes — cost-based selection in Step 2.2. Output partitioned into non-overlapping threshold bands.
Out: step_2_1_EF_{ISO}_{T}.parquet per band
2.2a — Cost Optimization
step2_2a_cost_optimization.py
For each (ISO, threshold): loads EF mixes ≥ target score. Evaluates 5,832 (non-CAISO) or 17,496 (CAISO) cost combos. 9-dim key: {Ren}{Firm}{Batt}{LDES}_{Fuel}_{Tx}_{CCS}{45Q}_{Geo}.
Phase 1: Static 2025 → cheapest per sensitivity.
Phase 2: Extract unique winners → demand growth sweep (L/M/H × 2026–2050). Wright’s Law FOAK→NOAK.
Out: step_2_2a_CO_{ISO}.parquet
2.2b — Track NB / CTR
step2_2b_track_nb_ctr.py
New-Build Track: Only new resources (no existing credit).
Cost-to-Replace Track: Replace existing nuclear with new clean.
Demand growth sweep with Wright’s Law learning. P10/P50/P90 cost envelopes.
Out: track_scenarios.parquet → Step 4.1e/4.2c
Step 3 — Dispatch Cache & Marginal Abatement Cost Queue~10–30 min
3a — Dispatch Cache
step3a_build_dispatch_cache.py
Pre-computes full 8,760-hour dispatch for every unique mix from Step 2.2. Uses dispatch_utils.reconstruct_hourly_dispatch with detailed=True.
4-phase storage: bat4 → bat8 → LDES → H&sub2;. SOC carried across window boundaries, round-trip efficiency per discharge event.
Capacity translation: Step 1–2.1 stores capacity as % of annual demand. Step 3 converts: MW = (pct/100) × annual_MWh / duration_hours.
In: step_2_2a_CO_{ISO}.parquet, EIA profiles
Out: {ISO}_dispatch_cache.parquet, {ISO}_annual_manifest.parquet
3b — MAC Queue
step3b_mac_queue.py
Path-dependent deployment queue: per ISO × 5 price × 3 demand growth = 15 pathways.
At each threshold: dispatch floor → baseline CO&sub2; → sample archetypes → score new_build_cost / CO&sub2;_avoided → winner = argmin(MAC) ≤1% overshoot → ratchet floor.
Clean firm tranching: uprate → geothermal (CAISO) → min(nuclear, CCS). Cumulative caps tracked.
CO&sub2; model: Merit-order fossil retirement (coal → oil → gas). CCS residual: 0.036 tCO&sub2;/MWh.
Out: mac_queue_{ISO}.parquet, scenario_a_{ISO}.json

Step 4 — Derived Analytics~5–10 min
4.1a — Fossil Dispatch (CO&sub2; + LMP)
step4_1a_fossil_dispatch.py
Reads clean dispatch from Step 3a cache. residual = max(0, demand − clean). Merit-order: coal → oil → gas. CO&sub2; = ∑ dispatch × emission_rate. LMP = marginal cost of last fossil unit per hour.
4.1b–d — Profiles, MAC Stats, Targets
4.1b: 8,760h → 24h compressed day profiles (demand, matched, surplus, gap).
4.1c: Marginal MAC via PCHIP splines. 3 grid × 3 DAC = 9 crossover points.
4.1d: Optimal crossover threshold per ISO (grid MAC = DAC cost).
4.1e–f, 4.2a–c — Tracks & Storage
4.1e→4.2c: Export tracks → P10/P50/P90 cost envelopes.
4.1f: Building blocks — Step 1 generation shapes for dashboard.
4.2a: Resource density strips per scenario.
4.2b: Battery/LDES dispatch, seasonal storage, H&sub2; thresholds.

Step 6 — SMARTargets Market Simulation~20–60 min (sweep: hours)
6.1 — Market Simulation Engine
step6_1_smartargets.py
Market-driven clean energy deployment across 7 ISOs, 2023–2050. Reference (R1/R2): Profit-driven — deploy where revenue > cost (LMP + capacity + REC vs LCOE + TX + learning). Aspirational (AT1–AT4): Profit first, then mandated to meet emission cap; DAC backstop or queue overshoot. Quick Transition (QT1–QT4): 19 reduction targets (5–95% in 5% steps). Sweep: 270 scenarios = 2 conditions × 3 demand × 5 price × 3 PPA × 3 gas friction.
In: step_2_2a_CO, EIA profiles, pipeline_config   Out: smartargets_{scenario}.parquet, sweep_{type}_{ISO}.parquet
