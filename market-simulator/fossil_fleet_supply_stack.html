How to Read This Analysis

Understanding the Merit-Order Stack

Grid operators dispatch generators in order of marginal cost, with the cheapest and cleanest running first. The supply stack ranks every fossil plant by its CO₂ emission rate — a proxy for heat rate efficiency. The least-emitting plants run most hours; the highest-emitting only fire when demand pushes the price and need to the top of the stack.

Emission Rate = Stack Position

Lbs CO₂/MWh is a direct function of heat rate and fuel carbon content. Lower = more thermally efficient = dispatched earlier and more often.

Marginal Generator

The last unit called to meet demand sets the market clearing price. Plants above the marginal unit sit idle — those below are running and earning energy margins.

Displacement Effect

When CEG runs, it prevents higher-emitting plants from generating. Each MWh from a 820 lbs/MWh CC plant displaces ~1.3 lbs of CO₂ versus a 2,100 lbs/MWh coal unit.
