Physics Feasible Space (PFS)
Exhaustive 4–6D combinatorial search over resource fractions (solar, wind, nuclear, hydro, offshore, geothermal). Coarse grid (5% step): 12K–1.6M combos per ISO. Zone search (1% step): Fine grid in 3 score bands (50–70%, 70–90%, 90–100%). Floor-aware: Incremental additions above existing grid mix. Storage refinement: 3-pass adaptive sweep — bat4, bat8, LDES, H&sub2;. Each mix scored via vectorized 8,760-hour matching: HMS = ∑min(1, clean[h]/demand[h])/8760.
Out: {ISO}_coarse_cache.parquet, {ISO}_t{T}_raw_pfs.parquet, {ISO}_t{T}_storage.parquet
Efficient Frontier & Cost Optimization
EF extraction: Threshold gate → resource cap filter (solar 100%, total 350%, hydro existing) → global dedup. Cost optimization: Cross-evaluate EF mixes under 5,832 (non-CAISO) or 17,496 (CAISO) sensitivity combos. 9-dim key: {Ren}{Firm}{Batt}{LDES}_{Fuel}_{Tx}_{CCS}{45Q}_{Geo}. Cheapest mix per (ISO, threshold, sensitivity). Demand growth sweep with Wright’s Law FOAK→NOAK.
Out: step_2_1_EF_{ISO}_{T}.parquet, step_2_2a_CO_{ISO}.parquet

Core — Market Simulation
market_simulation.py
Entry point: run_market_simulation() (single), run_full_sweep() (1,215×). Flow: load_common_data() → build_market_scenarios() → build_single_scenario() × N → save_results(). Profit-driven: clean resources deploy where revenue > cost. Deployment stops at first unprofitable zone. Clean energy level is an OUTPUT, not a target constraint. New-build fossil: apply_economic_new_build() — RA trigger (reserve margin < 15%) + economic trigger. ORDC feedback loop: low reserves → scarcity spikes → new fossil viable → reserves restored.
In: EF parquets, EIA profiles, user params   Out: results/ (parquet, JSON, CSV)
LMP Engine + ORDC + Zonal
lmp_engine.py, zonal_lmp.py
build_merit_order_stack(): Sort fossil units by MC = HR × fuel + VOM + CO&sub2; × carbon. ISO-specific offer adders (10% capacity-market, 0% energy-only).
ORDC scarcity pricing: Exponential knee model — adder = 0 above knee, min(cap, VOLL × e^(−λR)) below. Per-ISO VOLL ($2K–$5K), knee_mw, cap.
VRE cannibalization: Capture-rate feedback — solar/wind revenue uses generation-weighted LMP, not average. Sigmoid depression at high VRE shares.
Zonal LP (zonal_lmp.py): Pipe-and-bubble, 2–5 zones/ISO. ORDC integrated into zonal path. Dual prices on balance constraints.
Demand response: Price-elastic curtailment (PJM 10 GW @$100, ERCOT 5 GW @$200). Off/Low/Med/High toggle.
LP Storage + Retirement
dispatch_utils.py
co_dispatch_storage_lp(): Rolling-window LP co-optimization of all storage types. scipy linprog (HiGHS) with sparse matrix constraints. Objective: minimize unmet demand.
Windows: 24hr (bat4), 48hr (bat8), 168hr (LDES), 720hr (H&sub2;). SOC carryover for multi-day.
Fossil retirement cascade: Coal → oil → gas CT → gas CCGT. Margin-threshold heuristic.
Nuclear retirement: Revenue floor (§45U PTC). Retires if energy + capacity + PTC < threshold.
Revenue Model
1. Energy: Hourly LMP × resource CF (capture-rate-adjusted for VRE).
2. Capacity: ISO-specific $/kW-yr, degraded by clean share.
3. Incentives: §45Y PTC ($26/MWh), §45U nuclear floor ($40/MWh, max $15 credit), ITC 30% (storage/offshore).
4. REC: Scarcity-driven: ACP × (1 − exp(−k × gap%)).
Cost Model + New-Build Fossil
LCOE: L/M/H × 7 ISOs × resource. Wright’s Law: C(Q) = FOAK × (Q/Q_ref)^(-b).
Transmission: Per-resource, per-ISO flat adders.
New-build fossil CAPEX: CCGT/CT/coal, L/M/H per ISO. Coal blocked in CAISO/NYISO/NEISO. Max build rate (GW/yr).
PPA discount: Regional market depth (ERCOT=1.0 → SPP=0.50).
Fleet Dispatch Engine
fleet_dispatch.py, fleet-dispatch-engine.js
Python: Plant-level economics across 1,215 sweep scenarios. Efficiency ratios, CCS ramp (0/2/5/8yr), economic retirement. Grid+fleet fossil reconciliation (additive rule). P10/P50/P90 envelopes.
JS port: Identical logic in browser. Instant recalc with fleet sidebar modifications. 200 plants × 1,215 scenarios × 6 years in <50ms.
Scenario Parameters & Sweep Grid1,215 scenario combinations × 7 ISOs × 6 years = 51,030 runs
Fuel & Carbon
Gas: $2–6/MMBtu (L/M/H). Coal: $2–2.50. Oil: $8–13.
Carbon: $0 (none), $5.50 (RGGI), $14 (full RGGI), $51 (EPA SCC), $100 (EU ETS), $185 (Rennert).
Clean Energy & Storage
Solar, Wind, Offshore, Nuclear, CCS LCOE (L/M/H per ISO).
Battery 4hr $295, Battery 8hr $456, LDES $220 ($/kW-yr defaults).
Geothermal: CAISO only, $63–$110/MWh.
6-Dimension Sweep Grid
3 demand × 5 price × 3 PPA × 3 gas friction × 3 queue cap × 3 NB fossil cost = 1,215.
Snapshot (single), Trajectory (6 timesteps 2023–2050), Sweep (full grid).
Tech-differentiated queue caps per ISO (LBNL Queued Up 2024).
Learning & Queue
Wright’s Law: nuclear 10–15%, CCS 10–12%, LDES 15–20%, battery 18–20%, offshore 8–12%.
2025 baselines: nuclear 2 GW, battery 50 GW, solar/wind 150 GW.
New-build fossil: NO learning curves (only clean resources learn).
